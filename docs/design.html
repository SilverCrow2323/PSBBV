Layout rules

Make every pixel earn its keep.

↔

Clear hierarchy

One dominant message per view. Supporting details stay supporting; they don't fight the title.

👆

Touch-sized targets

Touch works as a second input method. No pin-sized hotspots hidden between decorative lines.

🎯

Visible focus

Selection must be obvious through shape and contrast, not colour alone. Physical input remains first-class.

Typography & motion

Readable beats fancy.

  • Use the system font as the first native baseline; make the production typeface decision after hardware testing and licence checks.
  • Keep metadata short and labels readable at handheld viewing distance.
  • Motion communicates focus, hierarchy or state. It does not exist just to make the GPU sweat.
  • Use reduced-motion or immediate transitions when the setting is introduced.
  • Loading, offline, empty, planned, unavailable and success states must look meaningfully different.
